Free flow cytometry software

Several tools analyse FCS files at no cost. They differ in where they run, how much they do, and who they are for. This page compares the free options that are maintained and widely used.

Comparison

When to use FlowMate

FlowMate is built for plate-based experiments. A gate drawn once applies to every well, the plate shows each well's result, and conditions assigned on the plate carry through to graphs with the statistical tests done — the same tests and numbers as GraphPad Prism, including nested tests that use every cell without treating cells as replicates. It opens FlowJo 10 workspaces, and its results are validated against FlowKit, Beckman CytExpert, GraphPad Prism and R on published data.

For scripted pipelines, FlowKit, CytoFlow or flowCore fit better. For dimensionality reduction such as t-SNE in a browser, Floreada does it.
