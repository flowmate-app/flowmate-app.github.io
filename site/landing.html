How it works

Five steps, from FCS files to a figure. The demo plate in the app walks through them.

  1. LoadDrop FCS files or a folder. Wells are read from the file names.
  2. PlateSelect wells and name conditions. Mark controls.
  3. GateDraw or accept suggested gates. Every well updates live.
  4. MeasurePick populations and statistics, one row per well.
  5. AnalysisGraph by condition with t tests, ANOVA, Tukey or Dunnett.
